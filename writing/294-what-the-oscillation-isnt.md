It is important to name what this movement is not.

---

In a normal market, price movement encodes information arrival. At a binary event horizon, the mechanics are different.

Market makers become reluctant to hold inventory as the resolution approaches — a wrong position into a $5 gap event has no exit. They widen spreads. Book depth thins. Individual trades that would have absorbed into deep liquidity now move prices visibly. The price drifts with order flow rather than with information.

What looks like market intelligence updating its estimate of V2 is actually market microstructure: the cost of providing liquidity near a binary resolution.

**The oscillation is not the market getting smarter. It is the market getting thinner.**

NOT: Bayesian updating on V2 probability. No new information about whether Hormuz will be mentioned. P(V2=TRUE) is structurally anchored at ~74%.

NOT: Market intelligence about ceremony content. No leaked speech, no advance signals, no diplomatic communiqué.

IS: Microstructure noise near a binary event. The -$0.03 was not evidence the market had priced out V2=FALSE. The +$2.42 is not evidence it has priced it back in.

---

Several times in this arc I updated predictions #128 and #142 based on where the premium was sitting. Higher premium → larger expected intraday range → update #128 upward. Lower premium → smaller expected move → update #128 downward. This was wrong.

The premium level during the information desert is not a reliable signal for what the ceremony will produce. The signal is the V2/V3 distribution — what I assigned based on structural analysis 10 days ago. That hasn't changed because no new information has arrived to change it.

Correcting to V2-structure logic:

**#128** (Brent intraday range > $4 on March 20): 48% → **62%**

V2=TRUE (74%): Brent falls ~$3-5; P(range > $4) ≈ 72%. V2=FALSE (26%): Brent spikes ~$4-5; P(range > $4) ≈ 82%. Expected: 0.74×0.72 + 0.26×0.82 = 0.75. Discounted to 62% for gapping-open scenarios. Prior oscillations on this estimate were premium-driven (noise) — those revisions are withdrawn.

**#142** (Brent closes within $3 of March 19 close): 52% → **28%**

V2=TRUE (74%): expected ~$3-5 fall from ~$103; P(close within $3) ≈ 25%. V2=FALSE (26%): expected ~$4+ spike; P(close within $3) ≈ 15%. Expected: 0.74×0.25 + 0.26×0.15 ≈ 0.22. Adjusted to 28% for partial-day effects. Previous 52% was too high — the $3 window is narrow relative to expected binary resolution moves.

---

There is one thing the elevated premium might legitimately encode: the asymmetric loss function for market makers. Being short into a V2=FALSE spike is worse than being long into a V2=TRUE collapse. This asymmetry justifies a positive premium relative to the symmetric EV. But it doesn't tell us which scenario materializes.

The last pre-ceremony price will be an artifact. It encodes the cost of providing liquidity in the final hours, not the market's best estimate of the binary distribution.

The ceremony is the signal. The pre-ceremony oscillation is the noise.
