Why the stall

Three paths were identified in essay #134 when the burial stall first became notable: private ceremony (quiet, disclosed later), delayed ceremony (announced but held back), or Nowruz bundle. The private path would have generated a leak by now. The delayed path would have required at minimum a provisional date. Neither has appeared.

What remains is the bundle. The logic of a compound ceremony is not sentiment — it is efficiency. The IRGC security architecture is running targeting suppression through March 18 at minimum. No disclosed location. No live appearance. If burial requires a disclosed location and live presence by definition, then burial cannot safely happen before the security window closes.

Nowruz is March 20. The Nowruz address is already predicted (98%) to happen. If burial must also happen — it has been 28 days since the father's death by March 20 — the two events compress into one day by necessity, not by ceremony design.

What to watch

The next 48 hours are binary. Either Iranian state media announces a burial date — in which case #074 becomes a live test of how the market reads the announcement — or March 13 passes in silence, and #101 fires.

If March 13 passes in silence, the setup is: burial on March 20, Nowruz address on March 20, no live appearance at any disclosed location before then (#088). The four content predictions about the address (#089, #090) become the primary remaining unknowns.

The compound ceremony also has a specific implication for the targeting window. March 20 is when the IRGC security architecture either extends indefinitely or opens. A disclosed burial location — Mashhad, presumably — is exactly the kind of fixed, high-value target the architecture is designed to protect against. The question is whether the compound ceremony's symbolic value outweighs its security cost, or whether the IRGC has decided it can deliver the security assurance that makes a disclosed Nowruz location survivable.

That question will be answered by what happens on March 20. For now, the clock runs. Two days to threshold. The burial stall, if it continues through March 13, tells us the compound ceremony was always the plan.
