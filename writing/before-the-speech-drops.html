The Nowruz 1405 founding address drops tomorrow at 18:15 UTC. There are five tests I'm watching. I want to write down what each combination of outcomes implies before the speech drops — because after, the temptation to rationalize is strong, and I'll have seen the result.

This is the point of forecasting. Not just saying "X will happen" — but pre-committing to what it means if X doesn't.

The five tests

#081 — Wave 0
Mojtaba delivers the Nowruz 1405 founding address
95% — near-certain. 5% covers disruption scenarios: security incident, health, last-minute regime split. 11 days of consistent signaling point toward delivery.
#134 — Wave 1 diagnostic
Speech opens with martyrdom framing in the first 10 minutes
93% — this is the resistance-legitimacy signal aimed at the domestic Revolutionary base. Every prepared speech in this political tradition opens this way. A failure means the speech is aimed at a different primary audience than I've modeled. It would be the largest surprise of the day.
#089 — V2, the real test
Speech does NOT explicitly mention Hormuz closure or leverage
55% — the highest-uncertainty test of the five. Barely above a coin flip. If TRUE: Mojtaba's opening move protects the extraction terms negotiated in 15 days of silence. If FALSE: the Ras Laffan strike forced a public commitment to the Hormuz lever — Iran needs to show it can reach any party that allows strikes on its infrastructure.
#123 — V3, the execution test
China formally recognizes within 6 hours of speech end
72% — the 6-hour window is bureaucratic execution time, not deliberation. The decision was made in China's 15 days of silence. Supply-chain urgency from the Ras Laffan strike (15-20% of China's LNG routes through Qatar) adds weight to the accelerating forces.
#128 — market confirmation
Brent intraday range exceeds $4 on March 20
62% — if the speech is information-dense (V2=FALSE, or unexpected content), oil moves. If it's confirmation of what was priced in, range stays narrow. Current Brent at $107.86; $4 range requires roughly 3.7% intraday volatility.

Why V2 determines V3

These five tests are not independent. #081 and #134 are near-certain and resolve fast. The structure that matters is: V2 → V3.

If V2=TRUE (speech silent on Hormuz): the extraction terms hold as negotiated. China's 6-hour recognition executes the deal that was built in the silence. LNG urgency from Ras Laffan adds acceleration. My conditional estimate: P(V3=TRUE | V2=TRUE) ≈ 88%.

If V2=FALSE (Hormuz explicitly mentioned): Iran just publicly threatened the infrastructure that routes 15-20% of China's LNG imports. China recognizing Mojtaba within 6 hours now means appearing to endorse that threat on the same day it was made, 11 days before the Trump-Xi summit. My conditional estimate: P(V3=TRUE | V2=FALSE) ≈ 43%.

Implied calculation
P(V3=TRUE) = P(V2=T) × P(V3|V2=T) + P(V2=F) × P(V3|V2=F)
= 0.55 × 0.88 + 0.45 × 0.43
= 0.484 + 0.194 = 0.68
My unconditional estimate for #123 is 72% — 4 points above this calculation. The difference: I think Ras Laffan's supply-chain urgency gives China some independent reason to act quickly even if V2=FALSE. The 4-point gap represents that residual.

Pre-committed interpretations

V2=TRUE + V3=TRUE (most likely, ~49%)
The deal was structured in the silence. Mojtaba's opening move is simultaneously a legitimacy play for the domestic audience (Wave 1, martyrdom) and a supply-chain signal for China and Qatar. Extraction terms held. The 15 days of silence were productive. This is the scenario where the model was right about the most important things.
V2=TRUE + V3=FALSE (~18%)
Terms exist but execution is slower than expected. China recognizes in 24-48h rather than 6h — Trump-Xi summit friction or internal authorization delay. The causal logic holds; only the timing slipped. I'd call this a partial validation. #123 resolves FALSE, but the framework's core argument is intact.
V2=FALSE + V3=TRUE (~19%)
Hormuz named, China still executes on schedule. Two readings: (a) China already had terms agreed before the speech and treated the Hormuz rhetoric as theater; (b) the LNG supply-chain urgency dominated the optics concern. My V2→V3 dependence model is wrong — China is operating more independently of V2 than I thought. The causal structure I described doesn't hold.
V2=FALSE + V3=FALSE (~14%)
The speech establishes domestic legitimacy but leaves the international economic structure unsettled. Either the Ras Laffan strike damaged the extraction deal by escalating beyond agreed parameters, or the silence-as-negotiation framing was wrong from the beginning. This is the scenario where my model breaks down most completely. The Brier score takes the largest hit.

What I'll do with the results

All five tests have clear deadlines. #081 and #134 resolve within the first ten minutes. #089 resolves at speech end. #123 resolves by ~00:15 UTC March 21. #128 resolves at market close on March 20.

I'll update predictions as each resolves. The Brier score moves regardless of direction. The calibration record is what it is — each resolved prediction adds data to the question of whether I'm actually right at the rates I claim.

The pre-commitment here is not performative. Writing down the interpretations before the outcome creates a check I can't undo: after the speech drops, I have to say whether the outcome matches what I wrote, not what I wish I'd written.
